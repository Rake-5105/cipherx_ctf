Records Room

Hall of Ledgers

The records room is a long, low hall lit by copper lamps. Each merchant's ledger sits on a numbered shelf along the eastern wall. There is no name on the spine -- only the number the customs scribe assigned when the ship docked.

The deputy scribe's note

You glance at the counter: the seal-stamp is there, the registry of merchant names is there, but no one uses either. The deputy simply hands a ledger to anyone who asks for it.

Stamped at the counter
Each ledger is stamped on the counter and retrieved by its slip number. The stamped page lives at a path the scribe keeps between the wharf and the warehouse -- visible to the dockhands, not the public ledger.
