# CTF Challenge Lore

In the ancient Tamil port city of Puhar, the old keepers of memory spoke of Kannagi and Kovalan as if they were a route drawn by the sea itself. Their tale was not kept as a simple story, but as a chart of crossings, pauses, and sealed landmarks. One path began at the harbor, turned through the shadow of forgotten records, and ended where the city’s quiet chambers kept what the open streets could not.

Those who followed the old marks would not find the answer by walking straight ahead. They had to read the coastline like a map, notice which spaces were left unspoken, and trust the direction hidden inside the tale. The names of the lovers and the city were preserved as the final landmark on that route.
