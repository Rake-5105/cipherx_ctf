Sangam Memory

முன்னோர் வழி.

In the ancient Tamil port city of Puhar, memory was carried like a carved stone panel and a palm-leaf ledger. Kannagi and Kovalan were remembered along the same old route, where the harbor, the market, and the quiet inner chambers marked the way.

The answer is not placed in the open field. It sits where the old marks turn inward, where the map leaves a gap, and where the city keeps its most carefully folded memory.

Follow the marks, not the road.
