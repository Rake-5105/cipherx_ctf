# Ponni Kalanjiyam - The Vault of Karikala

## Flag Lore

The flag is framed as the recovered mark of Karikala's royal archive seal. According to the legend, the king entrusted the blueprints of the Kallanai dam to a bronze chamber beneath Ponni Kalanjiyam, where the seal of access was copied from river clay and temple metal. The vault's final lock does not guard gold; it guards proof that authority can be forged when trust is placed in an unverified mark.

In the story of the challenge, the plaintext flag represents the hidden line in the ledger: the secret name engraved after the forged seal successfully opens the inner chamber. It ties the ancient engineering heritage of the Cholas to the vulnerability itself, turning the bypass into part of the myth.
